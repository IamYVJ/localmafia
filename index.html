How to play ▾
The idea

The town hides a secret Mafia. Each night the Mafia quietly picks someone to eliminate; each day the whole table debates and votes to execute a suspect. Town wins when every Mafia is gone — the Mafia wins when they equal or outnumber the town.

A round
  1. Night. Everyone "sleeps." Players with a night role secretly act on their phone.
  2. Dawn. The host resolves the night and announces who, if anyone, was lost.
  3. Day. The survivors discuss out loud and accuse each other.
  4. Vote. The table votes; the player with the most votes is executed. Repeat from Night.
Roles

More roles unlock as the table grows (up to 10 players).

  • 🔫
    Mafia — at night, choose someone to kill.
  • 👮
    Police — investigate someone at night; learn Mafia or Innocent.
  • 😇
    Angel — protect someone at night, blocking a kill.
  • 🦷
    Dentist — silence someone so they can't vote next day.
  • 🎩
    Mayor — town leader whose vote counts twice.
  • 🎯
    Vigilante — one bullet all game; shoot someone at night.
  • 🃏
    Jester — neutral; wins if the town votes to execute them.
  • 🧑
    Citizen — no night action. Watch, discuss, vote.
